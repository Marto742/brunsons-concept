About

Built by a Wilmington native

Derek Brunson grew up in Wilmington, became a 3x NCAA All-American wrestler and fought for years in the UFC. He opened this gym to give the community the coaching he wished he'd had. Whether you want to compete, get in shape or learn to defend yourself, you'll get real instruction from people who've done it at the highest level.

FAQ

Before your first class

Do I need experience?

No. Most members started as complete beginners. Coaches will pair you with the right partners.

What should I bring?

Comfortable workout clothes and water. For kickboxing and Muay Thai, 16oz gloves and shin guards — we have loaners if you don't.

Do I sign a waiver?

Yes, everyone signs a waiver before stepping on the mat. Minors need a parent or guardian to sign.

Do you offer month-to-month?

Yes. Call or message us for current packages.

Can my kids stay busy while I train?

Yes — kids can watch shows or play under staff supervision while you're in class.
